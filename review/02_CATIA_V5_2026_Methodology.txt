slide2.xml: EFORCE METHODOLOGY MOTIVATIONNow you know basicsof 3D part modelling, but the model tree is not wellorganized,the part is not easilyeditable and in case of more difficultparts, it can be unstable (once you change an operation in the beginning of the tree, the rest of the part will crash). If you want to modify a part, which was designed by someone else, it can be very hard to find out, which sketch or parameter of an operation you need to change.A model with hundreds or thousands of parts is very complex. Properly designed and stable parts are much easier for the CAD program to handle, which means smoother performance in CATIA. It is like your workplace: when it’s chaotic, no one knows what to do. On the other hand, when it is organized, you can find things quickly and don’t have to waste energy searching for what you need.
slide3.xml: EFORCE METHODOLOGYFor this reasons we unified the process of designing a part and the organization of model tree.Similar methodologies are used by many engineering companies.Remember your mum telling you to tidy your room, otherwise you wouldn’t get dinner? You don’t have to listen to her, but you’d better follow thismethodology.
slide4.xml: CATIA INSTANCESProduct (Assembly):groupofsubassembliesorparts, usedformanipulationwithproductswithinthefirstlower level in model treePart: every single part (every part alone – single custom part, single bolt, single skin of CF part etc.), containsPartBodies, GeometricalSets, OrderedGeometricalSets, Drawingsetc.PartBody (PB): containerfor solid geometryGeometrical Set (GS):containerforwireframe geometryOrderedGeometrical Set (OGS): hierarchical (orderdependant) containerforwireframe geometry
slide5.xml: TERMINOLOGYLive model: Containsalloperationsforbuildingthe car. Everyoperationiseditable. -> usually catia modelsDead model: Containsonlyone element – result solid or geometry. Youcanaddoperations, but youcannotchange any previousbuildingoperation. Usually imported models or copied live model as deadCopy: Copy of element withalloperationslinked to theoriginal element. Ifyouchangeorigin, copy WILL change as well. Ifyouchange copy, origin WILL change as wellCopy As Result: Dead copy ofan element NOT linked to theoriginal element. Ifyouchangeorigin, copy WILL NOT change.Copy As ResultWith Link: Deadcopy ofan element linked to theorigin. Ifyouchangeorigin, copy WILL change as well. Duplication: Independent copy oforiginal element. Ifyouchangeorigin, copy WILL NOT change. Ifyouchange copy, origin WILL NOT change.

slide10.xml: CAR AXIS SYSTEMCar Axis System (CAS): Theabsolute axis systemofthe car isusuallyplaced on the axis ofthe front (sometimesrear) axleofthe car. In EFORCE Prague Formulateam we use the axis system on the front axle.X axis: against the direction of travel of the carY axis: to therightsideofthe carZ axis: vertical, pointing upLEARN THE AXIS SYSTEM BY MEMORY – IT IS USED FOR CAR DESCRIPTION VERY OFTEN EVEN OUTSIDE OF THE MODEL!!!

slide12.xml: PART AXIS SYSTEMPart Axis System (PAS): Every part has itsown axis system, which has exactlydefinedpositiontowards to theCAS. Every part has oneCAS and onePAS.Part Axis Systemis a geometrical set created in every part and contains:CenterPointAxis SystemThreeplanes: XY, YZ, ZXItsposition and orientationcan (usuallyis) bedifferentthanCASMotivation:Every part in the model carrytheinformationofitspositionwithinthe part itself (independent on externalparts).
slide13.xml: REFERENCESReference isrelationbetweenelements.In CATIA existsFather/Child reference.Child element cannotexistwithoutFather element.Referencingbetweentwopartsisunsafeforstable model – ifsomeonedeleteormodifyFather to element in your part, your part willcrash.Referencesbetweenparts are also big burdenfor model, so the model willbeslow and unstable.NEVER REFERENCE OUTSIDE OF THE PART ITSELF!!!
slide14.xml: REFERENCESFor positioning the part in whole car model always use PAS.Onlyforparts such as fasteners (and fewotherexceptions) itisallowed to use Snap. Snapisfunctionthatmoves part (contact, axis coincidenceetc.) without any link.References as assemblyconstraints are onlyallowed in kinematics model whichis independent to themain model.NEVER REFERENCE OUTSIDE OF THE PART ITSELF IN MAIN MODEL!!!
